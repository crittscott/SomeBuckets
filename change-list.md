# Player-visible changes

## Since 0.8.3

- **Mob Bucket ↔ vanilla mob buckets:** Using a Mob Bucket while holding a water-based vanilla or modded mob bucket (Bucket of Cod, Bucket of Axolotl, etc.) in the other hand moves that mob into the Mob Bucket and leaves a Water Bucket. Holding a Water Bucket instead moves the Mob Bucket's oldest mob out into its own mob bucket, if that mob has one. Only what the vanilla bucket format records is kept, and the moved mob gets a new UUID. Filling a mob bucket this way counts toward bucket advancements such as Tactical Fishing.
- **Shiny Mobs support:** Mob Buckets holding Shiny Mobs creatures are tinted with the matching vanilla mob's spawn-egg colors instead of gray, and Shiny Mobs buckets (Bucket of Shiny Cod, etc.) exchange with the Mob Bucket like vanilla mob buckets. The shiny Wither cannot be captured, like the vanilla one. Resource packs can give other egg-less mobs borrowed egg colors or explicit colors through `assets/somebuckets/mob_bucket_colors.json`.
- **Sushi Go Crafting colors:** Mob Buckets holding Sushi Go Crafting shrimp or tuna, which have no spawn eggs, are tinted in their own colors instead of gray.
- **Wooden and golden buckets no longer turn vanilla:** Hand-to-hand transfers ignore Meadow's wooden water and milk buckets and Caverns & Chasms' golden buckets, which would otherwise come back as plain vanilla buckets. Data packs can add other containers to the new `somebuckets:held_transfer_excluded` item tag.
- **Junk and Trash Bucket tempting:** Holding a Junk or Trash Bucket in either hand tempts mobs that would follow a player holding any of its stored items.
- **Mob Bucket "No room to release":** When a sneak-release fails because the mob would collide with a block, the action bar now says "No room to release" instead of nothing happening. This matters most for mobs wider than a block, such as Alex's Mobs frilled sharks.
- **Mob Bucket tag renamed:** The entity tag of mobs the Mob Bucket cannot capture is now `somebuckets:uncapturable` (was `somebuckets:mb_blacklist`), and it also includes the loader boss tags (`#forge:bosses`, `#c:bosses`). Data packs that extended the old tag must use the new name.
- **Structure loot is tunable by data pack on both loaders:** Each bucket reward is its own loot table, `somebuckets:inject/<reward>`, so a data pack can change its chance, contents, or disable it. On Fabric, bucket rolls are now added even when another data pack replaces the target chest's loot table, as on Forge. See `docs/datapacks.md`.
- **Land claims through standard events:** Bucket actions now fire the loader's ordinary break, place, block-use, entity-interaction, and item-pickup events, so any claim mod that watches them protects its claims; FTB Chunks and Open Parties and Claims both do, without a Some Buckets adapter. On Fabric, claim mods using Common Protection API are consulted too.
- **Dispensers respect claims:** Dispensers fire the same events as the `[SomeBuckets]` fake player, so a claim mod's fake-player settings now stop dispensers from feeding, capturing, releasing, or moving items inside a claim, not just from changing fluids. Dispensers also no longer act outside the world border.
- **Mob Bucket captures villagers and other interactive mobs:** Right-clicking with a Mob Bucket now captures before the mob's own reaction, so villagers, wandering traders, horses, and similar mobs can be captured instead of opening trades or being mounted.
- **Dispensers fill cauldrons with powder snow:** A Big or Huge Bucket of powder snow in a dispenser now fills an empty cauldron in front of it, as water and lava buckets already did.
- **No more bucket duplication from stacks of empties:** Using a stack of empty Some Buckets (on fluid, a tank, a cauldron, a cow, a mob, items, or through a dispenser) fills one bucket and leaves the rest of the stack, as vanilla buckets do. Previously the whole stack could come back filled.
- **Held-transfer leftovers go to the inventory:** Extra results of a hand-to-hand transfer go into the inventory first and drop at the player's feet only when it is full.
- **Off-hand bucket transfers first:** Using another fluid container on air with a Big, Huge, or Source Bucket in the off hand transfers between them instead of using the container on its own.
- **Mob Bucket capture rules:** Leashed mobs and mobs owned by another player can no longer be captured; dispensers cannot capture owned mobs. Aquatic mobs play their own bucket sounds on capture and release, and released mobs keep the "from bucket" status vanilla gives them.
- **Dispensers don't tame:** A Junk or Trash Bucket in a dispenser no longer feeds untamed wolves, cats, parrots, and other tamable animals.
- **Junk and Trash pickup rules:** The buckets leave alone items dropped for a specific other player, and collected items count toward the player's pickup statistics. They refuse items that carry an inventory: bundles with contents, filled shulker boxes, loot-table containers, and, on Forge, modded backpacks.
- **Unloadable Junk contents are kept:** Stored items from an uninstalled mod, or beyond capacity, are set aside on the bucket (shown in the tooltip) instead of being lost, and return once they can be loaded. Malformed saved bucket contents are removed and logged when the item loads.
- **Placement protection checks the placed block:** Fluid and powder-snow placement is checked against the block actually placed, and a refused placement restores the world without dropping items.
- **Tanks and cauldrons:** Tank exchanges count only what the tank actually accepts or gives. Filling a cauldron counts toward the Cauldrons Filled statistic. Using a Source Bucket on a full cauldron of its own fluid plays the pour sound, as pouring onto a matching source block does.
- **Forge `FillBucketEvent`:** Listeners can still cancel Some Buckets' world bucket use, but an `ALLOW` result no longer replaces the bucket's own behavior.
- **Fabric `/reload` and client sync:** On Fabric, `/reload` rereads `config/somebuckets-server.json`, and the server's Source Bucket allowlist is sent to clients so they no longer predict uses the server refuses.
- **Diagnostic client commands:** `/sb fluids` reports how each fluid is colored in buckets, and `/sb eggs` reports each Mob Bucket's color source.
- **Mob Bucket ↔ Mob Bucket:** Using a Mob Bucket with another Mob Bucket in the other hand moves one mob between them, with its full state: the used bucket gives its oldest mob if the other can take it, and otherwise takes the other's oldest. Any mob a Mob Bucket can capture, such as a cow, moves this way.
- **Upgrade Aquatic squid buckets:** Upgrade Aquatic's Bucket of Squid and Bucket of Glow Squid now exchange with the Mob Bucket like vanilla mob buckets, in both directions, and a glow squid stays a glow squid.
- **More Mob Bucket colors:** Bumblezone and Wilder Nature mobs without spawn eggs are tinted in their own colors.
